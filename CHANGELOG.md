# Changelog

## 0.1.1

Fixes found by benchmarking against limma 3.68.5 on eight TCGA tumour-normal cohorts (`report/`).

- **`eBayes`: the moderated t no longer uses a clipped `stdev_unscaled`.** limma applies `stdev.coef.lim`
  only to the prior variance of the B-statistic; 0.1.0 clipped `stdev_unscaled` to [0.1, 4] before computing t.
  With voom weights, or with large samples or continuous covariates, many genes have `stdev_unscaled` < 0.1, so
  t was deflated (to 0.65x in TCGA-BRCA) and 4,107 genes across six TCGA cohorts lost significance at FDR 0.05;
  for a continuous covariate t fell to 8% of limma's. (Fixed earlier in the copy vendored in omicverse.)
- **B-statistic:** `eBayes` computes `lods` and `var_prior` as limma's `.ebayes` / `tmixture.matrix`;
  `topTable` reports `B` and sorts by it by default, as limma does, and accepts any `adjust_method`.
- **`lmFit(v, design)`** accepts the `EList` from `voom`, taking expression, weights and gene names from it.
- **Packaging:** `patsy` and `statsmodels` are declared dependencies; `import pylimma` failed in a clean
  environment with 0.1.0. `pylimma.__version__`.
- Tests: R-reference parity tests (`tests/test_r_parity.py`, regenerated by `tests/data/make_reference.R`);
  the test suite no longer ignores `RuntimeWarning`.
